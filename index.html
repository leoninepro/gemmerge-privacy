Privacy Policy for Gem Merge

Last updated: [August 9, 2026]

This Privacy Policy explains how Gem Merge ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Gem Merge itself does not require you to create an account and does not collect any personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

The App stores your high score locally on your device. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party.

2. Advertising

Gem Merge displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob, a service operated by Google LLC. To deliver and personalize ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and process certain information automatically, including but not limited to:

  • Advertising identifiers (such as your device's Android Advertising ID)
  • Device information (device model, operating system version)
  • General location information (derived from IP address, not precise GPS location)
  • App usage and interaction data related to ads (e.g. ad impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google's own privacy policy, available at: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can review and adjust your ad personalization settings at https://adssettings.google.com, and on most Android devices under Settings → Google → Ads.

3. Children's Privacy

Gem Merge is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your information. The only third party involved in data processing is Google (via the AdMob SDK), as described in Section 2 above.

5. Data Retention

Your locally stored high score remains on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its data. We do not retain any server-side copy of this information, as we do not operate a backend server for this App.

6. Your Choices

You can limit ad tracking at any time via your device's system settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization on most Android devices, or the equivalent "Limit Ad Tracking"/"Allow Apps to Request to Track" setting on iOS).

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
